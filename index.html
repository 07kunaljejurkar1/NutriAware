🌿 About NutriAware

NutriAware is a nutrition and healthy-food awareness project designed to help people explore balanced meals, healthier food choices, recipes and basic nutrition concepts.

Our objectives

  • Promote awareness of balanced nutrition.
  • Encourage healthier everyday food swaps.
  • Help users explore simple recipes.
  • Provide approximate nutrition education and progress summaries.

NutriAware is an educational project, not a replacement for professional medical or dietetic advice.

🥗 General Healthy Eating Suggestions

  • Include protein sources such as dal, beans, eggs, milk, curd, tofu, or paneer.
  • Choose a variety of grains, including whole-grain options.
  • Eat different coloured vegetables and seasonal fruits.
  • Choose fibre-rich foods such as oats, beans, vegetables, fruit, and whole grains.
  • Reduce excess added sugar and highly salted packaged snacks.

Estimates are not a medical diet plan. Children and teenagers need age-specific guidance. People who are pregnant, have medical conditions, or need therapeutic diets should consult a qualified healthcare professional or dietitian.

🔄 Healthy Food Swaps

Try practical changes without giving up every favourite food.

🥤 Sugary soft drink→💧 Water or unsweetened drink
🍟 Fried chips→🥜 Roasted chana
🍞 Refined bread→🌾 Whole-grain bread
🍬 Sweets every day→🍎 Fruit more often
🍜 Instant noodles alone→🥦 Add vegetables and protein
🍚 Only refined grains→🌾 Try oats or whole grains
🍪 Biscuits as a snack→🥛 Plain yogurt or fruit
🍗 Deep-fried foods often→🔥 Baked, grilled, or steamed options
🧂 Very salty snacks→🌰 Unsalted nuts or seeds
🧃 Sweetened packaged juice→🍊 Whole fruit and water
🍕 Meal with few vegetables→🥗 Add a side salad or vegetables
🍫 Frequent sugary snacks→🥜 Try nuts or roasted pulses

Food swaps are flexible suggestions. Dietary needs, allergies, affordability, and personal preferences matter.

🥗 Healthy Recipes

Simple meal ideas using everyday ingredients.

🥣

Vegetable Poha

Ingredients: Poha, peas, onion, carrot, lemon.

Method: Cook vegetables with spices, add rinsed poha, steam briefly, and finish with lemon.

🥗

Chana Salad

Ingredients: Boiled chana, tomato, cucumber, lemon.

Method: Mix the ingredients, add lemon and spices, and serve fresh.

🥞

Moong Dal Chilla

Ingredients: Soaked moong dal, onion, spices.

Method: Blend dal, mix in spices, and cook thin pancakes on a lightly oiled pan.

🥛

Fruit Yogurt Bowl

Ingredients: Plain yogurt and seasonal fruit.

Method: Add chopped fruit to yogurt. Extra sugar is usually unnecessary.

🥪

Vegetable Sandwich

Ingredients: Whole-grain bread, tomato, cucumber, paneer.

Method: Layer vegetables and paneer between bread slices.

🍲

Dal Vegetable Soup

Ingredients: Dal, carrot, tomato, spinach.

Method: Simmer ingredients with water and spices until tender.

🌾

Vegetable Dalia

Ingredients: Broken wheat, peas, carrot, beans.

Method: Cook the grains and vegetables with water and mild spices until soft.

🫘

Rajma Rice Bowl

Ingredients: Cooked rajma, rice, tomato, onion.

Method: Simmer rajma in tomato gravy and serve with rice and vegetables.

🥔

Sprout Chaat

Ingredients: Properly prepared sprouts, tomato, onion, lemon.

Method: Mix ingredients and season to taste. Cook sprouts thoroughly when food safety is a concern.

🥣

Oats Porridge

Ingredients: Oats, milk or fortified alternative, fruit.

Method: Simmer oats in milk or water and top with fruit.

🫓

Besan Chilla

Ingredients: Besan, onion, tomato, coriander.

Method: Make a batter with water and vegetables, then cook as a pancake.

🥬

Palak Dal

Ingredients: Dal, spinach, garlic, spices.

Method: Cook dal and spinach until soft, then season to taste.

🔄 Healthy Food Swaps

Small changes can help you build healthier eating habits.

🥤 Sugary soft drink→💧 Water or unsweetened drink
🍟 Fried chips→🥜 Roasted chana
🍞 Refined bread→🌾 Whole-grain bread
🍬 Frequent sweets→🍎 Fresh fruit
🍜 Instant noodles→🥗 Vegetable-rich meal
🍚 Only white rice→🌾 Try varied whole grains

You don't need to avoid every favourite food. Aim for variety and balance.

🥗 Healthy Recipes

Simple meal ideas for everyday life.

🥣

Vegetable Poha

Ingredients: poha, peas, onion, carrot, lemon.

Steps: Rinse poha. Cook vegetables with spices, add poha and steam briefly. Finish with lemon.

🥗

Chana Salad

Ingredients: boiled chana, tomato, cucumber, lemon.

Steps: Mix the ingredients, add lemon and your preferred spices, and serve fresh.

🥞

Moong Dal Chilla

Ingredients: soaked moong dal, spices, onion.

Steps: Blend dal with water, mix in spices, and cook thin pancakes on a pan.

🥛

Fruit Yogurt Bowl

Ingredients: plain yogurt and seasonal fruit.

Steps: Add chopped fruit to yogurt. Avoid adding extra sugar if not needed.

🥪

Vegetable Sandwich

Ingredients: whole-grain bread, cucumber, tomato, paneer.

Steps: Layer the vegetables and paneer between bread slices and serve.

🍲

Dal Vegetable Soup

Ingredients: cooked dal, carrot, tomato, spinach.

Steps: Simmer the ingredients with water and spices until tender.

Understanding your nutrition

Healthy eating includes variety, suitable portions, vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and protein sources. Nutrition needs are different for each person.

ℹ️ About NutriAware

NutriAware is a nutrition and healthy food awareness project designed to encourage informed food choices.

🎯 Project Objective

To make basic nutrition information easy to understand and help users develop healthier eating habits.

🌍 Sustainable Development Goal

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being. The project promotes nutrition awareness as part of a healthy lifestyle.

✨ Main Features

Balanced meal planning, food swaps, recipes, nutrition lookup, quizzes, water tracking, and activity analytics.

⚠️ Important Note

This is an educational demo. Nutrition values are approximate. It does not provide medical advice, real AI image scanning, or personalised clinical recommendations.
